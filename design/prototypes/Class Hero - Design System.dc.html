Design System · Version 001 · July 2026
CLASS
HERO
A visual system for the school-parent app. Composure of a fashion house — negative space, hairline rules, considered type — applied to the interaction grammar parents already know: a feed, a grid, a story, a bottom bar of five.
Art direction — luxury monochrome
Interaction — social convention
Platform — iOS first
01
Principles
Space is the luxury
Never fill a screen. A single card with 64px of air above it outranks four cards competing. Density is for utilities; this is not a utility.
Rules, not boxes
Separation comes from a 1px hairline and whitespace. No shadows, no rounded cards, no filled chips, no gradient.
Type carries the tone
Didone display for the one thing that matters. Light grotesque, wide-tracked, uppercase for everything structural. Nothing bold, ever.
Imagery is cropped, not framed
Full-bleed to the screen edge or to a hard square. Faces off-centre, generous crop. Never a rounded thumbnail floating in padding.
