Class Hero · Tappable mockup
Ten screens,
one bottom bar
Instagram's interaction grammar — feed, stories rail, 3-up grid, five-tab bar, full-screen viewer — drawn in the house style: monochrome, hairline rules, Didone display, cropped imagery, and a great deal of nothing.
Tap the bar to move between the five destinations. Tap a circle on the feed to open Stories — timed full-screen notices from the school, canteen, uniform shop and each class. It advances itself, swipe or tap across for the next, swipe down to return to the feed, and the last one hands you back to the feed. Rings go from ink to hairline once seen.
The other signature moment is Photo Consent in the Vault: withheld by default, every image of your child blurred everywhere until you grant it.
